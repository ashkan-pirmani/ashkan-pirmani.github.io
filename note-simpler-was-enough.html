When a simpler method was enough

A transformer where logistic regression did the job, and a pipeline that Excel already covered.

1 minute read.

The transformer

I once presented a complicated transformer architecture. My instinct was that a more sophisticated model had to give us something better.

What I tried
Logistic regression, on the same task.
What surprised me
It did the job. And it was easier to understand, and to explain to the people who would use it.
What I did next
We went with logistic regression. The model had to be deployed and used, not only shown in a conference paper.

The spreadsheet

Another time, I was explaining a pipeline to a team. Convert the data to Parquet, then process it through several stages. The team lead stopped me. Excel already did what the team needed.

The right size

As engineers, we tend to reach for the most impressive tool. I did, both times. But impressive is not the point. If a simple model does the job, and the people who use it can understand it, that is more than fine.

You do not need a spacecraft to commute between two small towns. And you do not need a container ship to send one small parcel.

So now, before I choose a method, I ask how long the trip is, and how big the parcel is.

Let's talk.

If your question depends on health data that can't simply be put in one place, I probably want to hear about it.

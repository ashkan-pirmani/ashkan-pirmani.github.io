Experience

2026 to now

Innovation Mandate, drug safety methods

UHHasselt University, with Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine as industry partner. Belgium.

  • Won a personal Innovation Mandate grant from the Flemish government's innovation agency (VLAIO), with Johnson & Johnson Innovative Medicine as industry partner.
  • Developing methods that keep two treatment groups comparable in propensity score analyses when each data source has few patients: first in multiple sclerosis against criteria set in advance, then on Optum's US health records and insurance claims. The plan.
2024 to 2026

Postdoctoral researcher

KUUHKU Leuven, Department of Electrical Engineering, and Hasselt University, Data Science Institute, jointly funded. Belgium.

  • Personalizing a federated model to each of 32 countries improved ROC-AUC by 7.2% and AUC-PR by 31% over the same model without personalization, and beat pooling all records, on 26,246 patients. First author, npj Digital Medicine (2025).
  • FLoRank, my personalized federated method built on adaptive low-rank factorization, improved AUC-PR by 24% over standard federated learning and F1 by 8% over LoRA, on registry data from over 26,000 patients at 146 centers. Prototype. ECTRIMS personal grant for scientific quality (2025).
  • FLkit, an open guide for teams starting studies across centers that keep their own data: 29 contributors, supported by ELIXIR Belgium. I led its design. First author, arXiv (2026).
  • Identified five barriers that stop research software from being reused, from scope to institutional trust, by retiring my own framework, FL4E. First author, Medical Informatics Europe (2026).
  • Organizing team of OHDSI Europe 2025. Invited talks at Roche, the Flower community and the North American Society of Artificial Intelligence in Multiple Sclerosis.
  • Coordinated with data holders, clinicians and legal teams in more than thirty countries. Supervised master's students.
2019 to 2024

Doctoral researcher

KUUHKU Leuven and Hasselt University.

  • Added 3,527 records, 45.5% more, to a worldwide study of multiple sclerosis medicines and COVID-19, through a federated route I built for registries that could not send data: a Docker container at each registry turned its records into grouped counts. Its findings informed the global COVID-19 advice for people with multiple sclerosis. First author, JMIR Medical Informatics (2023).
  • Designed the collection's data pipeline: one core data set agreed across 18 registries, three routes for data to come in, and the same quality checks on every stream.
  • In over 5,500 people with multiple sclerosis, the anti-CD20 medicines ocrelizumab and rituximab were associated with more severe COVID-19 than glatiramer acetate, and interferon-β was not protective compared with other medicines. My part was data acquisition, the pipeline and the federated route. Shared first author, Neurology: Neuroimmunology & Neuroinflammation (2022) and Multiple Sclerosis and Related Disorders (2022).
  • Led the disability progression study, from the data access proposal to MSBase and the ethics approval to the experiments. Designed AdaptiveDualBranchNet in Python and PyTorch, and ran it with Flower and Ray on the Flemish Supercomputer Center's cluster.
  • ATHENA, a public-private partnership with Janssen Pharmaceutica, funded part of the PhD. Worked on the analysis in its machine learning work package.
  • Built FL4E, a framework for federated studies, and the degree of federation, tested on classification and on survival analysis. On a public classification benchmark, letting half the centers send their records kept 97.5% of the fully federated score. First author, JMIR Formative Research (2024).
  • Taught in the Data Science in Healthcare course at Hasselt University, and began supervising master's theses in 2021.
2017 to 2019

Research assistant, Decision Support Systems Lab

IDIranDoc, Iranian Research Institute for Information Science and Technology, Tehran.

  • A simulation of Iran Post's national parcel network in R and NetLogo, searched with a genetic algorithm for a better set of hubs, found a layout with 15% lower projected transport cost, in simulation.
  • Delivered as a decision support dashboard and a policy analysis for senior executives.
